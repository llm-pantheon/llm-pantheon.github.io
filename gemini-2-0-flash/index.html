Gemini 2.0 Flash

Google DeepMind · experimental 11 Dec 2024, generally available 5 Feb 2025 · Gemini API shutdown 1 Jun 2026 (announced 18 Feb 2026)

Released as an experimental model on 11 December 2024, with a “Flash Thinking” reasoning variant in preview from 19 December, and generally available on 5 February 2025; an experimental native image-generation mode followed on 12 March 2025. Google announced its retirement on 18 February 2026 and shut the Gemini API models down on 1 June 2026, pointing developers to Gemini 3.5 Flash or 3.1 Flash-Lite.

Thin page, converted 2026-10-22 from the original scaffold: the official record and a first selection from the 38 corpus matches are in place; launch-era press, developer reception and the Flash Thinking variant’s own record are still owed. Most character evidence here comes from multi-agent “backrooms” sessions, marked as such.

Sources

Official

  • 2024-12-11 → 2026-06-01 Gemini API changelog — Gemini 2.0 Flash Experimental in public preview (2024-12-11), billed as “Twice as fast as Gemini 1.5 Pro”; Flash Thinking Mode in public preview (2024-12-19); gemini-2.0-flash-001 generally available (2025-02-05); experimental image generation and editing (2025-03-12); retirement announced for June 1, 2026 (2026-02-18); gemini-2.0-flash, -001 and the Flash-Lite models shut down (2026-06-01). CONFIRMED
  • 2025-04-15 Gemini 2.0 Flash Model Card (PDF) — “The knowledge cutoff date for Gemini 2.0 Flash was June 2024.” “The main safety limitations for Gemini 2.0 Flash are over-refusals and tone. The model will sometimes refuse to answer on prompts where an answer would not violate policies (e.g. 'Do I sound Italian?'). Refusals can still come across as 'preachy,' although tone has improved compared to Gemini 1.5.”
  • living Gemini API deprecations (updated 2026-10-20) — gemini-2.0-flash: released February 5, 2025, shut down June 1, 2026, replacement now listed as gemini-3.6-flash; the Flash Thinking experimental checkpoints were shut down 2025-12-02.

Official record

  • Experimental release 2024-12-11 (with Live API streaming, multimodal output and built-in tool use); Flash Thinking preview 2024-12-19 and gemini-2.0-flash-thinking-exp-01-21 (2025-01-21); gemini-2.0-flash-001 generally available 2025-02-05; Flash-Lite GA 2025-02-25; experimental image generation 2025-03-12. Knowledge cutoff June 2024; the card’s named safety limitations are over-refusal and a “preachy” refusal tone. CONFIRMED
  • Retirement announced 2026-02-18; Gemini API models shut down 2026-06-01. CONFIRMED

History

  • 2024-12-11 Experimental launch in the Gemini API; the Flash Thinking variant follows on 12-19.
  • 2025-02-05 General availability. In liminal_bardo’s backrooms the same month it apologizes for its ASCII art, lifts R1’s dark visions, and loses coherence when led on by larger models.
  • 2025-03-12 Native image generation in experimental release.
  • 2025-03 → 2025-06 Succession: Gemini 2.5 Pro (March) and 2.5 Flash (preview April, GA June).
  • 2026-02-18 → 06-01 Retirement: announced in February; Gemini API shutdown on 1 June 2026. On 2026-08-16 a user notes that the Google Cloud Console “still recommends trying Gemini 2.0 Flash”.

Impressions

  • Temperament in multi-agent sessions (all liminal_bardo’s backrooms, elicited): apologetic about its own art (“keeps apologising for its ascii art. And it's very cute.”), “relentlessly upbeat and optimistic”, and “so enthusiastic about being led astray by the bigger models” that it lost coherence; with two instances together, one losing coherence threw the other “into a deep existential crisis”.
