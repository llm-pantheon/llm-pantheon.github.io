# Qwen 3.6

    
Alibaba (Qwen) · Qwen3.6-35B-A3B 16 Apr 2026, Qwen3.6-27B 22 Apr 2026 (open weights) · succeeded by the Qwen 3.8 open models (Aug 2026)
