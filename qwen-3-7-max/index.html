Qwen 3.7 Max

Alibaba (Qwen) · previewed on LM Arena, May 2026 · announced 20 May 2026 (closed weights) · succeeded by Qwen3.8-Max (Aug 2026)

Previewed anonymously on LM Arena and formally announced at the Alibaba Cloud Summit on 20 May 2026; unlike Qwen 3.5 and 3.6 it was kept closed-weight, and the Qwen 3.8 generation (July–August 2026) returned to open weights. In September 2026 Anthropic alleged that Claude transcripts had been distilled into Qwen 3.5, 3.6 and 3.7.

Thin page: release record, its place in the open/closed sequence, and the distillation allegation; fuller reception pending.

Official record

  • Announced 20 May 2026 at the Alibaba Cloud Summit (not the Apsara Conference, which is held in September): “Unveiled at the Alibaba Cloud Summit, Qwen3.7-Max is Alibaba’s latest large language model, engineered for advanced agentic coding, complex reasoning, and long-horizon task execution.” Closed weights; available through Alibaba’s cloud.
  • Previews: “Alibaba previewed two models simultaneously: Qwen3.7-Max-Preview and Qwen3.7-Plus-Preview.” (MarkTechPost). Z.ai’s GLM-5.2 launch table (16 June) benchmarks against Qwen3.7-Max. tk — Qwen3.7-Plus general-availability date (the earlier page said 1 June; unverified)

History

  • 2026-05 Arena first: “two preview versions of the Qwen3.7 series quietly appeared on Arena AI’s leaderboard with no press release and no official API announcement.” (MarkTechPost).
  • 2026-06→07 Open-weight competition: after GLM-5.2’s open release (16 June), one user’s reading: “They released 3.7 Max and noone cared because GLM5.2 could be downloaded” (Nymne, 2026-07-07).
  • 2026-07-19 Succession: Qwen3.8 announced as open-weight; Gigazine: “Alibaba released Qwen 3.5 and Qwen 3.6 as open models, but Qwen 3.7 was only released as a closed model. With Qwen 3.8, they have returned to an open model strategy.” See Qwen3.8-Max.
  • 2026-09-10 Distillation allegation REPORTED: Anthropic says harvested Claude transcripts “were used to distill Claude’s capabilities into Qwen 3.5, 3.6, and 3.7”, measuring “over 151 million exchanges observed” between May and July 2026. A competitor’s allegation. tk — Alibaba’s response, if any

Impressions

  • Standing: rated “on par with Sonnet 4.5” by a skeptic of Qwen benchmark claims (RealEverNever, 2026-07-19); Simon Willison, in August, called the sibling Qwen 3.7-Plus “one of Qwen’s strongest models of any size as recently as May this year”.
