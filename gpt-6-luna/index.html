GPT-6 Luna

OpenAI · released 22 Sep 2026 · small/fast tier of the GPT-6 generation · current

The small, fast tier of the GPT-6 generation, released 22 September 2026 with GPT-6 Sol — trained with the same methods as GPT-6 Astra and priced at $0.10/$0.50 per million tokens (a 50% cut from GPT-5.6 Luna’s promotional pricing). It is the free- and high-volume tier: the GPT-6 model Free and Go users reach, and the one labs and startups cite for cost-sensitive agent workloads.
