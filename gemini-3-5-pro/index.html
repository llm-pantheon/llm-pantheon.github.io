Gemini 3.5 Pro

Google DeepMind · announced 19 May 2026 (release promised for “next month”) · never publicly released as of 22 Oct 2026 — no Gemini API listing, model card or app release; no cancellation announced

Announced at Google I/O on 19 May 2026 alongside Gemini 3.5 Flash, with Google saying it was “already being used internally” and would roll out “next month”. It missed June and later reported dates; on 21 July 2026 Google said it was “currently testing with partners” and that pre-training for Gemini 4 had begun. Gemini 4 Argon was announced on 30 September 2026 without a 3.5 Pro release; as of 22 October no Gemini API listing, model card or cancellation notice for 3.5 Pro has appeared.

This page records an absence: the model never reached a public surface, so its evidence is the announcement trail, reporting on the delay, and Google’s later documents that pass over it. Reported partner and enterprise testing and brief arena sightings are tagged as reported. The tweet layer is ten corpus matches, most of them anticipation.

Official record

Google-published statements only, plus the absences in Google’s own documentation.

  • Announced 19 May 2026 at Google I/O as part of the Gemini 3.5 series, the series opening with 3.5 Flash; release promised for the following month (“we look forward to rolling it out next month”). No benchmarks, pricing, context window or model card were published for 3.5 Pro.
  • 21 July 2026: “Gemini 3.5 Pro is currently testing with partners and we plan to make it broadly available as soon as it’s ready.” The same post announced the start of Gemini 4 pre-training. This is the last first-party statement about 3.5 Pro found this pass; a Google spokesperson told Fortune in August that it “was currently in testing”. CONFIRMED
  • Absences. The Gemini 3.5 Flash model card (May 2026) and the 3.5 Flash-Lite card (July 2026) each ran their frontier-safety comparison against Gemini 3.1 Pro as “the most generally capable model as of publication of this model card”. The Gemini 4 Argon Safety & Security Report (October 2026) benchmarks Argon against “Gemini 3.7 Flash, 3.8 Flash and 3.1 Pro”. As of the Gemini API deprecations page of 2026-10-20 and the changelog, no 3.5 Pro endpoint was ever listed, and no cancellation has been announced. CONFIRMED

History

  • 2026-05-19 Announcement: Gemini 3.5 launches with 3.5 Flash as the default model in the Gemini app and AI Mode; 3.5 Pro is promised for June. OfficeChai later reports that the “next month” line “drew an audible groan from the audience, according to multiple reports from the event” REPORTED.
  • 2026-06 → 07 The missed dates: June passes; MarketScale (07-06), citing Business Insider, places the model in a limited Vertex AI enterprise preview REPORTED; Bloomberg (07-16, as cited by TechCrunch and InfoWorld) reports internal delays as Google struggled to meet performance goals REPORTED.
  • 2026-07-21 Flash releases instead: Google ships 3.6 Flash, 3.5 Flash-Lite and 3.5 Flash Cyber, says 3.5 Pro is testing with partners, and announces Gemini 4 pre-training; TechCrunch headlines the release “Google releases three new Gemini models — but no 3.5 Pro”. On the 22 July earnings call Pichai answers questions on competitiveness by describing Gemini 4 and a near-monthly release cadence (InfoWorld, 07-23).
  • 2026-08 Reorganization and the shelving report: Fortune (08-10) reports three missed deadlines, engineers attributing the delay to coding, and a leadership change at Google DeepMind (Demis Hassabis moving to a chairman role) REPORTED; the same day OfficeChai relays a SemiAnalysis note that 3.5 Pro was “quietly shelved” and that it “has surfaced on Arena a handful of times over the past few weeks, briefly appearing in blind testing pools before being pulled, in one case after less than an hour” RUMOR.
  • 2026-09-30 Succession without release: Gemini 4 Argon is announced (generally available 13 Oct). The Decoder reports 3.5 Pro “skipped entirely” REPORTED. Google has published nothing further on 3.5 Pro as of 2026-10-22.

Impressions

  • Anticipation: Zvi Mowshowitz in May, “Gemini 3.5 Pro is confirmed for next month”, and in July, “the one that counts”; PlastiqSoldier (2026-07-15) expecting it “this weekish”; whitenoisemachn (2026-08-01) allowing that Google “should be back with Gemini 4 even if 3.5 Pro disappoints.”
  • On the cause of the delay (all reported; Google has said only that the model was in testing): coding performance short of internal goals (Bloomberg via TechCrunch; Fortune’s engineers, “failing to prioritize AI coding abilities”); token efficiency and long multi-step reasoning (MarketScale, citing Business Insider). Testers who met it in arena pools, per OfficeChai’s summary of one thread, found outputs “adding a lot of surface-level features while lacking any real detail underneath” RUMOR.
  • tk — any first-hand partner or enterprise-preview report naming the model; the Bloomberg (2026-07-16) and Business Insider originals; the arena sightings’ dates and model aliases; any Google statement after 2026-08-10.
