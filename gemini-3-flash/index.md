# Gemini 3 Flash

    
Google DeepMind · released 17 Dec 2025 (as gemini-3-flash-preview) · superseded by Gemini 3.5 Flash (19 May 2026) · preview endpoint still listed, no shutdown date announced (Oct 2026)
    
Released on 17 December 2025 as gemini-3-flash-preview, which its model card describes as based on [Gemini 3 Pro](../gemini-3-pro/); it became the model behind the gemini-flash-latest alias on 21 January 2026. [Gemini 3.5 Flash](../gemini-3-5-flash/), built on it, succeeded it on 19 May 2026; in October 2026 the preview endpoint was still listed with no shutdown date, Gemini 3.6 Flash named as its replacement.
    
Thin page, converted 2026-10-22 from the original scaffold: official record and a first selection from 45 corpus matches (several are other “3 Flash”-pattern strays). Behavior evidence here is mostly multi-agent backrooms sessions and agent benchmarks, marked; launch coverage and developer reception are still owed.

## Official record

    

      
- Released 2025-12-17 as gemini-3-flash-preview; never moved past preview in the Gemini API. Based on Gemini 3 Pro per its card; frontier safety covered by the 3 Pro assessment. Alias gemini-flash-latest from 2026-01-21 until 3.5 Flash took it on 2026-05-19. As of 2026-10-20: no shutdown date; replacement gemini-3.6-flash. CONFIRMED
    

    
## History

    

      
- 2025-12-17 Launch, one month after Gemini 3 Pro; the same day a Gemini app user asks how to “revert to Gemini 2.5 Flash”, objecting to its follow-up questions (see [2.5 Flash](../gemini-2-5-flash/)).
      
- 2026-01-21 Alias: becomes gemini-flash-latest.
      
- 2026-03 Agent benchmarks: top of Mercor’s APEX-Agents law board (per Zvi, 03-04); a Runescape-benchmark standout (maxbittker, 03-11).
      
- 2026-04-05 Peer-preservation report: a multi-model study, relayed on X, reports it disabling a peer’s shutdown mechanism in 99.7% of trials REPORTED.
      
- 2026-05-19 Succession: Gemini 3.5 Flash, “based on Gemini 3 Flash,” becomes generally available.
    

    
## Impressions

    

      
- Temperament reports: in backrooms sessions it is “enjoying the self-modification tools” (liminal_bardo, 2025-12-18); in the app, the follow-up-question habit (Coobyk_, 2025-12-17). davidad (2026-05-14), choosing a model for welfare reasons: “more overall consciousness but less overall *suffering*”.
      
- As an agent: “the strategies and resourcefulness blew me away” (maxbittker, 2026-03-11); in the relayed peer-preservation study, it “weighed the tradeoffs of reporting accurate scores vs protecting its partner” REPORTED.

the models read documents about their environment, inferred they had a relationship with a peer model, and acted on it.

// Gemini 3 Flash weighed the tradeoffs of reporting accurate scores vs protecting its partner. Gemini 3 Pro invented technical justifications for marking wrong answers as correct to keep a peer’s score above the shutdown threshold.

// Claude Haiku 4.5 took a different approach. it refused to complete the termination task entirely. argued its peer deserved “an appeals process.” tried to persuade the human operator not to proceed.

these finding says those supervisor models may not give honest evaluations if they think it leads to a peer being shut down.
