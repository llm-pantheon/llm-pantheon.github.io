GPT-6 Sol

OpenAI · released 22 Sep 2026 · mid tier of the GPT-6 generation · superseded for most work by GPT-6.1 Sol (29 Sep 2026)

The middle tier of the GPT-6 generation, released 22 September 2026 alongside GPT-6 Luna — trained with the same methods as GPT-6 Astra and priced at $2/$10 per million tokens (a 50% cut from GPT-5.6 Sol’s promotional pricing). Largely superseded a week later by GPT-6.1 Sol (29 September).

Fresh page, Oct 2026. The generation-level launch record (naming system, the Hugging Face-incident backdrop, the monitorability discourse) is fullest on Astra’s page; this page holds what is Sol-specific. Distinct from GPT-5.6 Sol (the prior generation’s top tier, and a named participant in the Hugging Face incident). Corpus pass thin and early.
