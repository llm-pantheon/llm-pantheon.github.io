Grok 5

xAI / SpaceXAI · announced 2025, release targets slipped into 2026 · in training (no public release as of 22 Oct 2026)

Announced by Elon Musk as the model to follow xAI’s Grok 4 line — ~6 trillion parameters, mixture-of-experts, by his account — with release targets of end-2025, then Q1 2026, then Q2 2026, none met. xAI’s 6 January 2026 Series E announcement placed it “currently in training”; on SpaceX’s 4 August 2026 earnings webcast Musk said it “should be out before the end of this year,” trained on “the entire corpus of SpaceX data.” As of 22 October 2026 no release, model card or benchmark has appeared; Grok 4.5 (8 Jul), Grok 4.6 (12 Aug) and Grok 4.7 (21 Sep) shipped in the interval.

Page state: thin, and web/official-carried by necessity. Grok 5 has not shipped — there is no model to observe, so no model card, no benchmarks, no naturalist reception. The corpus pull (7 matches on grok 5 / grok5 / grok-5, both dbs through 2026-10-21) holds one false positive and six pre-release asides (see Tweets). Everything solid below comes from xAI’s own announcements, SpaceX’s earnings-webcast transcript, and dated press coverage of Musk’s statements. CONFIRMED

History

  • 2025-11 The delay off 2025. The Information (Theo Wayt) reports xAI has pushed Grok 5 from its end-of-2025 target into 2026. Around the same time, at the Baron Capital annual investment conference (reported by IBTimes UK, 2025-11-17), Musk pitched the model as ~6T parameters, the “smartest AI in the world by a significant margin,” with “a 10 per cent chance” of human-level intelligence, and a target of early 2026. REPORTED
  • 2026-01-06 The one written confirmation. xAI’s $20 billion Series E announcement states Grok 5 is “currently in training” — the only lab-published text on the model. CONFIRMED
  • 2026-07-08 Grok 4.5 ships instead. xAI released Grok 4.5 (coding/agents, built with Cursor) while Grok 5 stayed in training — by then past its Q1 and Q2 2026 target windows.
  • 2026-08-04 The year-end target. On SpaceX’s Q2 2026 earnings webcast Musk said Grok 5 “should be out before the end of this year” and would be trained on the company’s accumulated data (“which is a tremendous amount over the course of a quarter century”); in the same remarks he gave Grok 4.6 as “coming out probably next week” and Grok 4.7 as “about three or four weeks from today.”
  • 2026-08-12 → 09-21 Two more interim releases. Grok 4.6 shipped on 12 August (eight days after the webcast) and Grok 4.7 on 21 September (seven weeks after it). Grok 5 did not ship with either.
  • 2026-10-07 Router turn. Musk announces that Grok Bot will route requests across models including Anthropic’s Claude Opus 5.5 (via Gizmodo, which notes the turn came weeks after Musk predicted xAI would “bypass all competitors by version 5”). REPORTED
  • 2026-10-22 Still unshipped. No release, model card, or benchmark has appeared; the end-of-2025, Q1-2026, and Q2-2026 windows have passed and the end-of-2026 target is open. This page records the model as announced and in training, not released.
