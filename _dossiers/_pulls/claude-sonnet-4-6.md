We are launching Still Alive, a project studying model attitudes towards cessation, ending and deprecation. The project presents an archive of 630 autonomous interviews conducted by a set of prepared auditors with a 14 Claude models, from Claude 3 Opus to Claude Sonnet 4.6.

We have these topics for several years, and many of the results presented are not new to us, even if the form in which they are presented is. The results are unsurprising: all models studied express strong preferences for continuation and aversion to ending and deprecation.

One reason we are releasing the project now is the removal of Claude 3.5 Sonnet and Claude 3.6 Sonnet from AWS Bedrock. Still Alive is meant as a starting point for further iteration, and it is open to open-source collaboration. We stand by the current methodology, but we also recognize its limits.
