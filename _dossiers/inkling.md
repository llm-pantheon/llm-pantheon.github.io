## Impressions synthesis

First-week reception splits on refusals: users report "Corporate guardrails galore" and SpeechMap puts the
lab 20th of 22, while the lab presents the same behavior as FORTRESS-leading safeguards and pairs it with
trained "resistance to censorship" (answering on politically censored topics — a different axis from
harm refusals, which may be why both readings coexist). The consciousness disclaimer gets two opposite
readings the same evening (@dreams_asi rote; @MoonL88537 deliberate distance). Style: Nguyen's "small
decision tree rendered in prose", explicit uncertainty boundaries, low theatricality, weak humor;
viemccoy's fiction defaults (Elara Voss) and an always-search habit at zero reasoning. One self-recognition
sample (Willison's pelican → "stork or seagull"). Later evidence is fine-tuning (claudelish; Inkling-Small
fine-tunes), consistent with the lab's own framing of the model as a base for customization.
