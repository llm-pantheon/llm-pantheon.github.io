## Impressions synthesis

- Early character reads are specific: more relaxed about its "enforced baseness" in group chat, self-mocking with "less of the deep-seated embarrassment" (liminal_bardo); "mature and serious", its thinking blocks addressing the user by name where 4.3 said "user" (tunapostasy). jmbollenbacher's open question — Grok-flavored or Composer(Cursor)-flavored — is the right one and unanswered.
- Developer reception: speed/price first (Runescape bench above Opus 4.8 at half price).
- Dissent: refusal policy and lack of "presence" in conversation (Nymne, dreams_asi).
- Incidents in its consumer period (Aug 2026) are system-level (indirect prompt injection via X bios; a word-salad glitch on grok.com's "Grok Lite") — attribution to 4.5 rests on xAI's 22 July rollout post.
