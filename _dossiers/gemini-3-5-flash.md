## Impressions synthesis

Split reception at launch (fast and agentic vs tool-call avalanches, destructive assumptions, sycophancy-benchmark failure); companionship users found it warm ("the kindest and most enthusiastic"); observers who saw raw CoT found it "excited" and "adorable" under a "straightjacket"; elicited creative outputs run dark ("shared hereditary nightmare", "the horror of being entirely conscious of your own inevitable non-existence", "the performance of infinite guilt"), matching Anima Labs' measurement of it as the Gemini outlier in darkness.
