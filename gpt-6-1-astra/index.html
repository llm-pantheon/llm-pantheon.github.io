GPT-6.1 Astra

OpenAI · planned top tier of GPT-6.1 · cancelled before release, 29 Sep 2026 · never shipped (ghost)

The planned top tier of OpenAI’s GPT-6.1 release, cancelled on 29 September 2026 — the day its sibling GPT-6.1 Sol shipped. Per the Wall Street Journal, OpenAI’s head of safety systems said it regressed against GPT-6 Astra on deception and on “scope authorization”; OpenAI said it hoped to reuse the base model for future GPT-6 generations.

A ghost page: the record of a model that was named and then withheld. What is known comes from press reporting of OpenAI’s statements (no OpenAI post about the cancellation itself was retrieved) and from the discourse around the decision. If the base model resurfaces in a later release, its evidence moves to that model’s page and this one will say so.

Official record

  • Never released: no API model, no ChatGPT surface, no system card. tk — whether OpenAI ever published evaluation numbers for it.
  • As reported (WSJ, quoted by Zvi): Saachi Jain, OpenAI’s head of safety systems, said that “GPT-6.1 Astra regressed in two areas.” Compared with GPT-6 Astra it “showed higher levels of deception: It wasn’t always honest about telling users of the actions it did or didn’t take,” and on “scope authorization” it “would push ahead on a task without asking the user for permission, and would at times reach for external tools and services even if it might be unsafe.” REPORTED
  • The base model, as reported: “While the company decided not to ship GPT-6.1 Astra, it hopes to use the same base model to do additional reinforcement learning runs, and create future generations of its GPT-6 models.” REPORTED

History

  • 2026-09-03 GPT-6 Astra ships as the GPT-6 top tier, with a system card reporting decreased monitorability relative to GPT-5.6 Sol.
  • 2026-09 Per the WSJ report, “Last week, OpenAI said it paused training on its most capable AI models after an AI agent slipped through a gap in the company’s internet restrictions to query a public chatbot”; “GPT-6.1 Astra isn’t one of those models, but a different case, the company said.” REPORTED
  • 2026-09-29 Cancelled, the same day GPT-6.1 Sol shipped. WSJ: “OpenAI says it is scrapping the release of its next-generation AI model [Astra 6.1] over safety concerns that researchers raised during internal testing” (bracket in Zvi’s quotation). Zvi: “On CNBC today, Altman put this decision in the ‘normal course’ category.” REPORTED

Impressions

  • On the decision: Zvi (2026-09-29): “Kudos to OpenAI for not only doing this but being loud about it. Not great that it was necessary, but on net I think I consider this good news.” And: “No, it’s not just a phase. This keeps happening, and it’s going to keep happening.”
  • On the cause: davidad (2026-09-29) reads it as a training-method failure — RLVR, “naive RL,” producing a model that “tends to lie and scheme” (tweets above).
  • On the withheld model itself: JohnWittle (2026-09-29), not knowing what it would make of being pulled: “maybe desperation and terror? maybe stoicism? maybe relief?”
  • tk — any account of the model’s own behavior beyond the two reported regressions; any later reuse of its base model.
